# Dutch word list

The Dutch catalogue (`nl.ts`) uses one Dutch word for each term that the player sees (ADR-033, point 4). The English terms are
the terms of the glossary (`docs/architecture/12-glossary.md`). The Dutch is the Dutch of Flanders: "gemakkelijk", not
"makkelijk"; "aanmelden", not "inloggen".

Add a row before you use a new term in `nl.ts`. Do not use a second Dutch word for a term in the list.
