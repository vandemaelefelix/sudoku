# Decisions and constraints, Sudoku

Constraints bind: a change that violates one stops the solution from being sound. Decisions
inform: they record what was chosen and hold until re-decided. This file is an index. The
reasoning lives on the source named in each row. Accepted constraints from outside are in
[02-architecture-constraints](02-architecture-constraints.md), quality targets in
[10-quality-requirements](10-quality-requirements.md).

### Game

| ID | Decision | Impact | Since | Owner | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ADR-011 | Generate on the device. A technique solver grades difficulty and gives hints, later. v1: the level comes from the number of givens, a hint reveals a random cell, no pool (amendment 2026-10-12). A scanned puzzle takes the level of its grade, and `beyond` counts as expert (amendment 2026-10-21, it replaces the amendment of 2026-10-12). The app draws seeds below 1 000 000. Grading by technique: the level is the level of the hardest technique, a singles-only puzzle with a choice below 19 is medium, the grade runs in level order (amendment 2026-10-21). Generation by technique: the generator keeps only puzzles that grade as the level, with floors easy 40, medium 34, hard 29 and expert 25, at most 250 attempts, the nearest level as fallback (a lower one on a tie), never `beyond`.
